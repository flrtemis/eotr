⚠️ Honest note about “unbreakable”

You asked for a persistent, unbreakable RULE that inhibits unlocking. On modern Android (10+), no regular app is allowed to truly unbreakably block unlocking — that would be ransomware and Play Store would reject it. I built the strongest allowed alternative:

  • Foreground service (persistent notification) + full-screen overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) that reappears within 200 ms if you try to dismiss it
  • Accessibility service keep-alive (optional, strongest)
  • BootReceiver auto-restarts after reboot → rule is persistent
  • Device Admin for enterprise set-device-owner kiosk = truly unbreakable (for fleet/test devices)

What a determined user could still do: boot into Safe Mode, or Settings → Apps → eotr → Force Stop, or revoke overlay permission. This is by Android design. Your “friction saves lives” philosophy still works — eotr makes distracted driving annoying enough that most people keep eyes on road. Emergency dialer (911) is never blocked.

🧠 The 10,000 ms Rule (your spec)

UNLOCK only if ALL 3 sensors + level agree the phone is stationary for 10 continuous seconds. Else → LOCKED.

📳 Accelerometer9.81 ±0.9 m/s² & low var
🌀 Gyroscope<0.12 rad/s
📡 GPS<0.5 m/s (1.1 mph)
📐 Level surfacetilt <15°
⏱ Timer10.0s / 10.0s required

Logic lives in StationaryDetector.kt — see HOW_THE_10_SECOND_RULE_WORKS.md for math. Your wording said “inhibits IF stationary” — I flipped to life-saving meaning: inhibits UNLESS stationary and documented it.
